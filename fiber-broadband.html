Need to know

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Which is the best fiber broadband plan for my home?

Consider how many people use the connection, the number of devices and whether you mainly browse, stream, study or work from home. Our team can help you compare the plans available for your area.

Do your unlimited plans have hidden data caps?

Our home broadband plans include unlimited data. Ask our team for the full plan terms when you enquire.

How long does a new fiber broadband installation take?

Share your location and our team will confirm feasibility and the installation schedule for your address. Installation on our home plans is free.

Can I use fiber broadband for work and online classes?

Yes. Home broadband can support video meetings, online learning and cloud applications. The experience depends on the selected plan, your Wi-Fi setup and the number of devices in use.

Where can I check pricing and availability?

Send an enquiry with your area or city, or message us on WhatsApp. The team will confirm plan details, pricing and availability.

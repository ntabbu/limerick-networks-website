Refund Policy

How refunds, cancellations and adjustments are handled.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Scope

This Refund Policy applies to payments made to Limerick Network for internet leased line, corporate and home broadband, OTT and live TV, CCTV and home automation, and other services. The commercial terms in your order form or service agreement, where one exists, apply together with this policy.

2. Installation and one-time charges

Installation, registration and equipment charges are generally non-refundable once the installation or equipment delivery has been carried out. If we are unable to provide service at your location after you have paid, the amount paid for that service will be refunded.

3. Plan and subscription charges

  • Plan charges are billed in advance for the selected period.
  • If you cancel during a paid period, service continues until the end of that period; unused portions are generally not refunded unless your agreement states otherwise.
  • Where a service outage attributable to Limerick is not resolved within the committed service level, you may be eligible for a credit or adjustment as described in your agreement.

4. Duplicate or incorrect payments

If you have been charged twice or charged an incorrect amount, please write to us with the payment details. Verified excess payments will be refunded or adjusted against your next bill.

5. Refundable equipment deposits

Where a refundable deposit has been collected for equipment such as a router or ONT, it will be refunded after the equipment is returned in working condition, less any applicable charges for damage or non-return.

6. How to request a refund

Email support@limerick.co.in or call 020-48504814 with your name, registered mobile number and payment details. We will review your request and respond with the outcome.

7. Processing time

Approved refunds are returned to the original mode of payment. Timelines depend on your bank or payment provider, and are typically communicated to you when the refund is approved.

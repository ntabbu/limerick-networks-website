What is an Internet Leased Line?

A stable connection built around your business

An Internet Leased Line (ILL) is a dedicated connection reserved for your organisation, unlike shared broadband where performance can vary with local traffic. It helps teams access cloud applications, communicate with customers, transfer data and run business-critical systems with consistent connectivity.

From a single office to multiple branches, Limerick Network can help assess your bandwidth, uptime and security requirements and recommend a suitable solution based on location feasibility.

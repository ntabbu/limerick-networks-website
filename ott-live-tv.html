Helpful answers

OTT & Live TV FAQs

Quick answers to common questions before you get started.

What is needed to watch OTT content?

You generally need an internet connection, a compatible device such as a smart TV, phone, tablet or streaming device, and an account or subscription for the OTT service you choose.

Does Limerick Network provide every OTT app or channel?

Availability depends on the services and packages supported in your area. Contact our team to confirm current options; individual OTT subscriptions may be separate.

Can I watch on more than one device?

That depends on the OTT provider’s rules, your subscription and your internet connection. Tell us how many devices you use so we can help discuss suitable connectivity.

Will OTT or live TV be available at my address?

Options may vary by location and technical feasibility. Send an enquiry to check availability and the latest service details for your area.

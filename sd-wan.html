What is SD-WAN?

SD-WAN Solutions for Secure & Scalable Business Networking

SD-WAN is a software-defined approach to wide area networking. Instead of managing each branch link separately, you set rules once and the network decides which connection suits each application best.

It suits SMEs, corporate offices, enterprises and multi-location businesses in Pune and across India that rely on cloud tools, video calls and shared systems every day.

  • Offices, branches and remote sites on one managed network
  • Works with leased line, broadband and other supported links
  • Policies and visibility managed from a central point

Common questions

Have Any Questions On Your Mind?

What is SD-WAN?

SD-WAN stands for Software-Defined Wide Area Network. It uses software-based policies to manage and route traffic across connected business locations over supported links such as leased line, broadband or 4G/5G.

How does SD-WAN work?

Software chooses the most suitable path for each application in real time, based on the policies you set and the condition of the available links, all managed from a central point.

Why choose SD-WAN over a traditional WAN?

A traditional WAN usually relies on fixed, site-by-site links. SD-WAN adds software control, so traffic can be steered across available links, with centralised visibility and the flexibility to mix link types.

Is SD-WAN suitable for my organization?

It may be useful if you operate multiple offices, depend on cloud applications or want a more centralised way to manage connectivity. Contact us to discuss feasibility and requirements.

Can SD-WAN use different types of internet connections?

Many SD-WAN solutions work with a mix of supported connection types. The right combination depends on the platform, availability and your business requirements.

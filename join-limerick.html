NOC Engineer – L1

Full timePune0–2 years24×7 rotational shifts

Monitor network infrastructure, respond to alerts, perform basic troubleshooting and escalate incidents on time.

Responsibilities & skills
  • Monitor network devices, links, servers and services using NMS/monitoring tools.
  • Identify and respond to alerts, alarms and incidents; perform L1 troubleshooting.
  • Monitor bandwidth, link status, latency, packet loss and availability.
  • Create and update tickets; escalate unresolved issues to L2/L3 as per SOP.
  • Coordinate with field engineers, vendors and internal teams; maintain incident logs and shift reports.
  • Skills: TCP/IP, LAN/WAN, DNS, DHCP, VLAN, basic routing; ping, traceroute, nslookup; NMS and ticketing tools; willingness to work rotational/night shifts.
  • Qualification: Diploma / B.E. / B.Tech / B.Sc. / BCA / CCNA in Networking, IT or Computer Science. CCNA is an advantage.

NOC Engineer – L2

Full timePune2–5 years24×7 rotational shifts

Handle escalated incidents, advanced troubleshooting and RCA to keep ISP and enterprise networks highly available.

Responsibilities & skills
  • Perform L2 troubleshooting of routers, switches, firewalls, WAN links, leased lines and internet connectivity.
  • Troubleshoot OSPF, BGP, VLAN, STP and related protocols; analyse logs, latency, packet loss and link failures.
  • Handle escalations from L1; coordinate with ISPs, vendors and field engineers.
  • Prepare RCA and incident reports; resolve incidents within SLA; support changes, upgrades and migrations.
  • Maintain network documentation and guide L1 engineers.
  • Skills: strong TCP/IP, routing and switching; OSPF, BGP, VLAN, STP, DHCP, DNS, NAT, VPN; NMS and ticketing; MPLS/ISP experience preferred.
  • Qualification: B.E. / B.Tech / B.Sc. / BCA / CCNA in Networking or IT. CCNA / CCNP is an advantage.

Project Coordinator (Internet & Telecom)

2 positionsPune0–3 yearsInternet / Telecom

Own the end-to-end delivery of Internet and Telecom projects, from order to service activation, coordinating customers, vendors, field engineers and NOC.

Responsibilities & skills
  • Manage end-to-end Internet and Telecom projects from order initiation to service activation and closure.
  • Coordinate installation and commissioning of Fiber, FTTH, leased lines, MPLS, SD-WAN and broadband services.
  • Prepare project plans, schedules, milestones and resource requirements; track progress and timely completion.
  • Coordinate with NOC, field engineers, network teams, vendors, contractors and customers.
  • Manage fiber laying, last-mile connectivity, equipment installation, configuration, testing and commissioning.

  • Profile: any graduate (Electronics / Telecom / Networking is an advantage). Freshers with relevant technical knowledge and candidates with up to 3 years of experience may apply.

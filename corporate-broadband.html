FAQs

Corporate Broadband Questions

Quick answers to common business connectivity questions.

What is corporate broadband?

Corporate broadband is internet connectivity intended for business use, such as office work, cloud applications, video meetings and connected business systems. Available features depend on the service plan.

Can I get corporate broadband at my office address?

Availability depends on the local network and technical feasibility. Contact Limerick Network with your full business address so the team can check available options.

Can I use it for video meetings and cloud applications?

Yes, business broadband can support video calls, cloud tools and file sharing. The best option depends on the number of users, devices and your business usage.

How do I request a new connection?

Use the Enquiry button on this page and choose Corporate Broadband, or contact our team by phone or email to discuss your requirement.
